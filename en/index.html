What I can do for you

Editing is my main job. Motion, 3D and web development join in when the project needs them, without bringing in another supplier.

Editing for YouTube and social

Long-form videos and vertical content for channels and brands that publish regularly. I handle editing, scripts, motion graphics, colour and animation.

For example Tutto Motori, Mr. Beaver, Mr. Kangaroo and Urban Heroes.

Motion graphics and 3D

Titles, animation and 3D imagery in After Effects, Blender and Unreal Engine, to explain an idea or build an atmosphere.

For example Zodiac, L’ultima delle borracce, Silentropy and Ship.

Websites and interactive experiences

Websites, small games and augmented reality projects. I built this portfolio too, including the channels that update themselves.

For example Cyberpunk Academy, Future Vibes Space and Pope Tarot AR.

How we work

  1. You tell me about the project. Goals, audience, materials and timing.
  2. I send you a first cut. We collect feedback in one round.
  3. I polish and deliver. In the formats you need, ready to publish.

Cookies and external content

The portfolio code does not set cookies or save preferences in localStorage or sessionStorage. Language is determined by the page address. Fonts and covers are served by this website. YouTube loads only when you select a video or the showreel: the privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com) requests immediate playback without a second notice. This mode does not guarantee the absence of cookies or data processing during playback. Instagram remains blocked until you select its consent button in the gallery. You can browse project pages without activating players or follow links to the providers.

Consent and withdrawal

The Instagram consent button authorises loading the Meta service and its possible use of cookies and identifiers under its policy (GDPR Article 6(1)(a) and Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code). YouTube loads directly when a video is clicked; no separate cookie consent is collected. IP address, device information, referring page and interactions may be shared. The choice exists only in memory while the gallery is open: closing it or reloading the page removes the player and does not retain consent. “Disable external content” in the footer removes an active player. Withdrawal does not undo prior processing; use your browser settings to remove cookies already set by the provider. Provider identifiers and their lifetimes may vary: consult the policies below.

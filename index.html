Case Study · 01 / Motion

Zodiac — a motion piece built to set a mood in seconds.

A self-initiated motion graphics project: an opening sequence where type, animation and 3D had to lock into one atmosphere from the very first frame.

01 · The Challenge

A look, not just an animation

With no brief and no client, the only goal was atmosphere. Every element — typography, motion, 3D — had to feel authored by a single hand, or it would read as a showreel instead of a world.

02 · The Approach

Designed end-to-end

Because I handle the motion design, the 3D and the grade myself, I built every layer against the same look from the first keyframe — no hand-offs, no compromises between stages.

03 · The Result

One coherent signature

A sequence that reads as a complete visual identity, not a collection of effects — a personal benchmark for the tone and finish I bring to client work.

About

Questions, answered

Before you even ask
What do you need from me to start?+
A short description of the goal, where the piece will live (YouTube, Instagram, TV, event screen…) and a rough deadline. If you have references you love, even better — I'll turn that into a clear scope and quote.
How fast do you deliver?+
It depends on scope, but speed is the point of a one-person pipeline: no hand-offs means no waiting. Social edits move in days, not weeks; bigger productions get a precise timeline in the quote — and I hit it.
Do you work remotely?+
Yes. I'm based in Catania, Italy, and shoot on location when needed — but editing, 3D, grading and development happen with clients anywhere. Reviews run through shared links with timecoded feedback.
How do revisions work?+
You see a first cut early, so surprises die young. Feedback rounds are agreed upfront in the quote, and because I'm the one who shot, edited and graded it, changes happen fast — no waiting on a chain of people.
Can you handle a project end-to-end?+
That's the specialty. Concept, shoot, 3D, motion graphics, edit, color, sound, delivery in every format — one brief, one person responsible for the result.

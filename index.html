Case Study · 01 / Motion

Zodiac — an opening sequence that sets the whole tone.

A title / opening sequence where edit, motion graphics and 3D had to land a mood in the first ten seconds. Built end-to-end, from concept to the final grade.

01 · The Challenge

Mood in ten seconds

An opening has one job: set expectations instantly. Type, motion and 3D had to feel like a single hand — or the sequence would read as a showreel, not a world.

02 · The Approach

Owning the full chain

Because I design the motion, model the 3D and grade the result myself, every element was built against the same look from the first keyframe — no round-trips between departments.

03 · The Result

One coherent signature

A sequence that reads as authored, not assembled — proof that a single-author pipeline is a creative advantage, not just a budget one.
